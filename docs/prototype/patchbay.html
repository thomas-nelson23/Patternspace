Patternspace Patchbay

A working prototype of the chosen layout. Everything in the window is live: wire modules, tag ports, split a patch across tabs, zoom and pan, switch presets and save subsystems. Press Sound in the top bar to hear the sample players.

Workspace tabs

Tabs above the workspace split a patch into parts. + adds one and a double-click renames it. Drag modules onto a tab, or select some and pick Move to tab. Wires to modules left behind turn into tags, so nothing stops playing. Tags reach across tabs, and a ↗ marks one that does. Broken Techno has a Patterns tab and a Kit tab.

Zoom and pan

Drag across empty workspace to draw a selection box (hold shift to add to the selection). Right-drag pans, as does the middle button, both from anywhere; scroll and two-finger drag pan too. Pinch or Ctrl-scroll zooms, and clicking the minimap jumps there. Fit frames the whole tab, and + − 0 work from the keyboard. Each tab keeps its own view.

Drum map

A generator modelled on Mutable Instruments Grids. Drag the dot around a map of grooves to morph between them: straighter at the top, more broken at the bottom, sparser on the left, busier on the right. A fill for each of kick, snare and hat sets how many hits play, the brightest hits are accents, and Chaos varies the pattern every loop.

Switch

A Switch cycles one input through 2 to 8 outputs (1 → N), or lets 2 to 8 inputs take turns at one output (N → 1). Each trigger at step moves it on one position and rst sends it back to the first. The lit number shows where it is. Switch Study uses both.

Sample player and 8 audio outs

A Sample player turns triggers into sound. Each Audio out picks one of the plugin's 8 stereo outs, which show up as separate tracks in your DAW, and meters its own level. Audio cables are thick, with square ports.

MIDI out

A MIDI out sends notes to your DAW on one channel. Set how many Outputs it has, then click an output to name it and pick its note, or choose a GM drum. Each output has its own input, so one MIDI out can hold a whole kit or just the parts going to one track. Double-click its name to rename it.

Tags

Click any port to give it a tag. An input with the same tag as an output is connected without a wire, like signal names in Q-SYS Designer. Hover a tag to light up everything on it, or turn on Tag wires to draw the hidden links on the current tab. The tag menu also sets a tag's color.

Text and blocks

T Text drops a note and ▭ Block draws a section, or wraps the selected modules. Drag a block's title to move everything inside it, drag its corner to resize, double-click the title to rename, and click the dot to pick a color.

Presets

The preset menu switches between whole patches, tabs included. Five are built in. Save in the menu stores your current patch as your own preset in this browser.

Subsystems

Drag a box around modules or shift-click them, then Save as subsystem. It lands in the library, where you can add it to any preset. Open ▸ goes inside one; Snare engine in Broken Techno is an example.

Editing basics

Click a library item to add it. Drag an output port onto an input to wire it. Click a wire or select modules and press Delete to remove them. Ctrl/⌘ C, X and V copy, cut and paste modules with the wires between them; a paste lands at the mouse. Every generator has clock and reset inputs, ▾ collapses a module, and ‹ › change values.
